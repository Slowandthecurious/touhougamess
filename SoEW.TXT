  ■1. Touhou Fuumaroku Backstory

      Here in the mountains of an eastern country, Hakurei Shrine was relatively peaceful.
    The shrine maiden of Hakurei Shrine, Reimu, had been training in the mountains to
    increase her spiritual power.
      Her training(?) was going well, but when Reimu suddenly said she wanted to sleep
    in a futon, they decided to return to the shrine for the time being.
      However, when they returned to the shrine, it had been infested with massive amounts
    of youkai, and other non-human things, weirdos, etc.

      Reimu: "What is this? I can't sleep peacefully like this! Perfect, I'll show you
    the fruits of my training!"

      However, training-hating Reimu had gone into the mountains for the hidden hot springs
    commonly found in mountains and delicious autumn flavors, so she hadn't really trained
    much at all.

      Genjii: "Milady, these youkai are strangely well-organized. Which means there must
    be a ringleader somewhere. Also, I sense some kind of foreign power... Anyway, I suppose
    I'll go with you."

      Genjii the turtle, Reimu's servant, said this while worrying about Reimu.

      Reimu: "Even if you hadn't said that, I was planning to bring you along. I can't
    fly by myself..."

      And so, Reimu took Genjii, and as usual with the Hakurei Shrine's greatest treasure,
    the Yin-Yang Orbs, headed to the Hakurei Shrine to exterminate youkai and investigate
    the cause.
      She would meet various strange people in various strange places...

      Reimu: "It's super weird (in Asagaoka Eriko's voice)"






  ■2. System Requirements

    PC-98 or compatible machine exclusive. (EGC equipped models)
    Runs on 386 or higher, but may be tough without a 486. Actually,
    fast VRAM access is most important.
    Options can reduce heavy processing effects.
    Also, MSDOS is required.

    CPU has only been tested on 486 (66MHz), so very slow models
    might not work well.
    By the way, on 486 (66MHz) there's no slowdown or missing elements.
    Being a shooter, slowdown makes it easier, but during gameplay it can
    become very hard to see at times, which isn't good for mental health.

    FM sound source (86 compatible) is nice.
    MIDI sound source (SC-88 compatible) is quite nice.

    If the game won't start, try freeing up some memory.
    Actually, 520k free should be enough.

    When running in DOS under Windows, don't use in a DOS window, use
    fullscreen.

    Of course (^^;, back VRAM is not saved, so disable any TSRs using
    it before starting the game.






  ■3. How to Start
    Execute GAME.BAT and the title screen will display 6 options:
            START       ・・・ Start the game.
            EXTRA START ・・・ Start Extra stage. (Conditions apply)
            HISCORE     ・・・ View current high scores.
            OPTION      ・・・ Change various settings. (See below)
            MUSIC MODE  ・・・ Listen to music and read comments.
            QUIT        ・・・ End game and return to DOS.

    Always start from the batch file. Don't start from the main program.







  ■4. Character Introduction

    ★  Protagonist ... Hakurei Reimu

          Shrine maiden of Hakurei Shrine. Born with spiritual power but lacks
        training. She attacks with cards and 'Spirit Strike' by releasing spiritual
        power. She exterminates youkai with the power of the 'Yin-Yang Orbs'.
          Her personality is optimistic, and even in emergencies like this, she
        doesn't worry at all, thinking her spiritual power will handle it somehow.

          This time, she fights (for some reason) riding a turtle.

    ★  Turtle ... Genjii

          A turtle that has acquired various divine powers through a long life.
        Captured during Reimu's training and worked to the bone. Possesses the ability
        to fly freely using the Flight Hermit technique.

    ★  Yin-Yang Orbs ... Hakurei Shrine's greatest treasure

          Orbs made of a mysterious mineral filled with spiritual power. At first glance
        just ordinary orbs that don't seem like weapons, but when used by someone of
        Hakurei blood, they emit terrifying spiritual power.
          This time, two Yin-Yang Orbs are attached to Reimu's left and right.





  ■5. Game Content

      A vertical scrolling shooting game set in the mysterious world surrounding
    a shrine maiden.
      Defeat enemies, collect items, and dodge bullets. (^_^;
      A shooter with bullet counts and hitbox size that would surprise even DoDonPachi.
      Don't be overwhelmed by the bullets...
      All 5 stages (+ 1 stage).





  ■6. Controls

      ● Player movement

                Cursor                  Numpad

                  ↑                     7  8  9

                ←   →                   4     6

                  ↓                     1  2  3


      ● Z Key      Fire card shots.
                    Holding down auto-fires, but rapid tapping slightly
                    increases fire rate.
                    Can defeat bosses faster.

      ● X Key      Use Spirit Strike.
                    The so-called bomb. Grants instant invincibility.

      ● ESC Key    Pause.
                    Press ESC again during pause to resume.

                    You can also quit the game from the pause MENU.

      During name entry:

          Z key to confirm one character.
          X key to delete one character.
          Enter or ESC key to finish.






  ■7. About Items

      P     ・・・ Power-up. Many appear. Collecting many
                  increases spiritual power.

      Point ・・・ Score. Various clear bonuses are multiplied.
                  On HARD, LUNATIC, EXTRA: 2x, 4x, 8x respectively.

      B     ・・・ Spirit Strike. Can hold up to 5. Don't miss them.

      S     ・・・ Greatly increases spiritual power. Don't miss it.

      1up   ・・・ Adds one Reimu. Max 5. Missing this means death.






  ■8. Config

      RANK  ・・・ Difficulty.
                  EASY        Easy shooting game.
                  NORMAL      Normal difficulty. Slightly easier than
                             arcade games. About 2x harder than EASY.
                  HARD        More bullets.
                             Just right for arcade shooters.
                  LUNATIC     Has revenge bullets. 2nd loop level
                             difficulty.

                  4 levels available.
                  Default is NORMAL.

      MUSIC ・・・ Sound source setting.
                  MIDI (MPU), FM, or off (no music)
                  can be selected.
                  MIDI requires SC-88 compatible device with MPU.
                  MIDI slightly reduces speed, so caution on slow machines.
                  Also, MIDI still uses FM sound source for sound effects.
                  MIDI alone won't produce sound effects.
                  Default is FM (if sound source available).

      PLAYER・・・ Number of Reimus.
                  Choose from 1 to 5.
                  More players is easier, but bonus points drop.
                  Default is 3.

      BOMB  ・・・ Number of Spirit Strikes.
                  Choose from 0 to 3.
                  Default is 3.

      Effects ・・ Choose normal or partially reduced.
                  If no slowdown, choose normal.
                  If normal is heavy and scrolling is rough, partially reduced
                  should improve it somewhat.
                  Especially Stage 4 is heavy, so if Stages 1-3 slow down,
                  you might want to choose partially reduced.
                  Game balance doesn't change.

      RESET ・・・ Returns all config to default settings.

      MUSIC MODE ・・・ Listen to music used in the game.

      QUIT  ・・・ Exit config screen.





  ■9. Other Information

      About Extend (1up)

        Extends at 1 million, 2 million, 3 million, 5 million, and 8 million.

        When reaching these points, if already at max lives (5), Spirit Strike
        usage increases by 1. If already holding 5 bombs, nothing happens.


      When lives reach zero...

        Only when lives reach zero does Spirit Strike usage increase by 2. Even
        if you extend after this and die again reaching zero, it increases by 2,
        so don't give up and keep trying.


      About Clear Bonus

        Bonuses include difficulty, stage, bomb, and miss.

            Difficulty  Easy        ・・・        0 points
                        Normal      ・・・    2,000 points
                        Hard        ・・・    4,000 points
                        Lunatic     ・・・    6,000 points

            Stage       0 points ~ 6,400 points
                       Based on no-miss time, continue count, etc.

            Bomb        2,500 - Uses this stage × 500

            Miss        3,000 - Deaths this stage × 1,000

            Initial Bombs   (4 - Initial bomb setting) × 800

            Initial Reimu   (5 - Initial player setting) × 1,000


          The sum of all above multiplied by the number of point items
          collected in that stage becomes the clear bonus.





  ■10. Touhou Project

      How did you like Touhou Project 2nd Work, Touhou Fuumaroku?

      I (ZUN) call this the Touhou Project, and I want to create games set in a
    world surrounding shrine maiden Hakurei Reimu - not ordinary shrine maiden
    games (?), but games with a slightly strange world.

    ● Touhou Project Works

      The memorable 1st Work
        Touhou Reiiden ... Single-screen action shooting (with puzzle elements)
                          Reimu challenges battles in Makai and Hell.
                          Hardcore difficulty, unusual systems, etc.
                          Recommended for unconventional gamers.

      The memorable 2nd Work
        Touhou Fuumaroku ... This work. Vertical scrolling shooter.
                          Hitbox smaller than even DoDonPachi would be surprised by,
                          and massive bullet counts.
                          Special effect bullets like lasers and reflecting bullets
                          not seen in DoDonPachi make for high visual impact.



      Future plans (48% lies).

        Touhou Yumejikan (tentative) Miko miko side-scrolling action.
                          Whether side-scrolling is (technically) possible is a mystery.
                          But I want to try making it.

      Also, falling block puzzles, versus fighting, MIDI music collection (haha), etc.
      Something with lots of movement would be good.

        Some people might (or might not) understand from the ending atmosphere,
      but the author (ZUN) is a Takemoto fan.
      I want to continue making games with that atmosphere but hardcore gameplay.




  ■11. Touhou Q&A

    Q: Game won't start, why?

    A: Possible causes: Not a PC-98 (^^;, insufficient memory,
       incompatible TSR, etc.
       To avoid these: Run on 98 or compatible. Free up about 520k
       main memory. Disable TSRs as much as possible. If still failing,
       program or data may be corrupted.
       Keep this game in one directory separate from other data.


    Q: Game started but won't START. Why?

    A: Probably memory shortage. (Or a bug (^^;)


    Q: Game started but no sound. Why?

    A: Do you have FM sound source? This game won't play BGM with only
       BEEP sound. Also, music is made for OPNA, so OPN produces
       somewhat sad sounds. (;_;)


    Q: No sound effects. Why?

    A: Do you have FM sound source? This game won't produce sound effects
       with only MIDI sound source.


    Q: High score seems weird.

    A: If a bug occurred mid-game but the game continued and recorded
       a high score, strange data may remain.
       In that case, delete HUUHI.DAT in the same directory. Scores
       will be reset.



    Q: Bullets are hard to see~, do something.

    A: Hmm, on notebook LCDs, fast bullets might be hard to see. Also,
       debris when defeating enemies (just 1-dot points) are almost
       invisible. Can't do anything about this.


    Q: Why shrine maidens?

    A: Mystery (^^; but shooters with space and mechs are common.
       However, shooters with this kind of world tend to be comical,
       with large characters and bullets, few bullets but hard to dodge,
       so I dared to make a hardcore shooter with shrine maidens. Enjoy
       this gap.


    Q: Myu~?

    A: We cannot answer that question.
