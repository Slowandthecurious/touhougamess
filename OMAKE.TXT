○Marisa's Touhou Fuumaroku Strategy Guide ====================================

    Yaho~, it's Marisa. Have you all gotten good enough to beat me no-continue?
  If you say you can't, you'll never beat Mima-sama.
    So, I'll teach you strategies and hints for this game~.

  ●About Reimu's Types
      Reimu's combat type can be chosen from three at start. The types
    from left are High Mobility Type, Defense Type, and Attack Type. Let me
    briefly explain each.

      High Mobility Type  ・・ Highest movement speed, so hard to miss items.
                              Also easiest to dodge bullets I think.
                                Shot doesn't fire anything from options even when
                              you get them (laugh). Instead, Reimu's cards are
                              wide and cover a broad range.
                              Power up increases bullet count nicely.
                                But worry is still firepower. That's
                              still a bit low. So boss battles may take a while.
                                But at max power, it's different. After all,
                              the two big forward cards actually penetrate. Super
                              powerful too. So maintaining max power is the
                              key point for this type.

      Defense Type      ・・・ Defense doesn't mean you don't die in one hit, so what's
                              strong? Bomb performance is high. Bomb power and
                              invincibility time are strong.
                                Movement speed is normal, and shot is balanced too.
                              Meaning it's a balanced type.
                                Actually, shots from options have better performance
                              than Attack Type, though fewer in number.
                                At max power, search shot is added making it somewhat
                              stronger I think.
                                This type is overwhelmingly easiest to clear.

      Attack Type       ・・・ This type has literally the most power.
                              Lowest movement speed so tends to miss items. Though
                              slow, this game itself has high movement speed standard,
                              so it's actually easier to handle.
                                But actually not much power at low power level.
                              Meaning, gets strong in latter half. Max power is too
                              strong, so when that happens, just don't die.
                                Can defeat bosses fast is the appeal.


    That's the type explanation, but to summarize:
      #1 for earning points is High Mobility Type.
      #1 for easy clear is Defense Type.
      #1 for easiest boss battles is Attack Type.
    Conversely, the bottleneck for all-character clear is probably High Mobility Type.

  ●About Bombs
      It's not bombs, it's Spirit Strike, I know.
    So, supplementary explanation about bombs (she doesn't get it).

      Bombs make you invincible the instant you press. Meaning, if you pressed
    bomb but died, you died before it traveled from brain to finger.

      Bomb power is minimal, but if you use invincibility to get close and shoot,
    it becomes decent power.

  ●  STAGE 1
      Stage 1 has no particular problems, right? If anyone dies from bullets during
    the boss laser, enter between the two lasers. It's a safe spot against bullets.
    If the following bullets look undodgeable, feel free to use a bomb.

  ●  STAGE 2
      What to watch out for in Stage 2 is not taking bullets thinking they're items
    in the latter half. These bullets accelerate straight down, meaning they fall
    just like items. Be careful.

    Boss is a bit strong if you're not careful.
    ・For bullets after the first cut, dodge by moving little by little so you don't
      get cornered, feels good.
    ・During V-shape or diamond, random bullets come out. Be careful. Use bomb when
      dangerous.
    ・Final phase has reflecting bullets. Trajectory is harder to read than it looks,
      so may be tough sometimes. Basically stay at the bottom and dodge big with only
      left-right movement. Even dodging by a hair is dangerous because of reflection.

  ●  STAGE 3
      Surprisingly many people struggle with Stage 3.
      ・Round small fry that shoot bullets have all aimed bullets. Pull them in and
        move, works well.
      ・Mid-boss has many bullets but zero random elements. Meaning, you can always
        dodge the same way. Try finding an easy way to dodge. Bomb works too though.

      Difficult parts of Stage 3 boss:
      ・First, green bullets from both ends. When switching, move big once and a gap
        appears making it easy to dodge.
      ・Next, when middle closes again, first carefully dodge white bullet scatter,
        then left-right green pincer bullets come. For this, stay in middle, and when
        it gets bad, escape outside. White bullet scatter comes immediately so be careful.
      ・When middle opens again, laser attack comes. When it comes from the edge, be in
        middle, and by the time thin line appears from middle, escape to edge. Then
        staying at screen edge is OK.
      ・After that, it's spirit.

  ●  STAGE 4
      Stage 4 mid-boss attacks randomly from the following:
          1. Single bullet straight down.
          2. Random bullet scatter downward
          3. Homing bullets
          4. Laser
          5. 5-WAY

      Consider 1, 2, 3 lucky.
      4 needs care. Won't hit at screen edge, but especially watch out with slow types.
      5 is probably difficult. Can only say watch carefully.

      Stage 4 boss, meaning me. I'm strong.
      Attack order is random so you have to respond after seeing.
      Basically destroy bits as fast as possible when attached. Attacks are fiercer
      when bits are attached. However, when all 4 are destroyed, sometimes strong
      attacks come. Be careful.
          When bits are attached:
          1. Main body ramming attack
          2. Spinning white bullet scatter
          3. Star rain
          4. Spread bits and attack
          5. Spread only 2 bits and bullets from bits
        Random from these.

      ・For 1, rotate around screen matching boss and it's OK. When you destroy all bits
        at this time, it changes to 3-WAY from main body but dodge the same way.
      ・For 2, looks easy when you watch carefully. But if boss is too close, bomb works.
      ・For 3, this attack alone isn't that scary, but depending on the next attack,
        sometimes can't dodge. Bomb perhaps.
      ・For 4, time it and enter the ring of bits. The safe spot for following bullets
        from main body is slightly left or right from directly in front.
      ・For 5, also enter between the two bits. After that, as the ring shrinks,
        simultaneously the remaining 2 bits' ring grows so escape outside immediately.

      Here, during 3, or when stars remain on screen, if you destroy all bits,
      bullets increase dangerously. Be careful.

  ●  LAST STAGE
      Last boss is divided into 5 phases.

      1. White bullet 16-direction tiers + 5-WAY
      2. Ramming + 16-direction bullets
      3. Giant spinning bullets
      4. White bullet scatter in rotation
      5. White bullets in fan from left and right, then 3-WAY in middle
      6. Giant spinning bullets, burst
      7. High-speed 3-WAY in fan from left and right
      8. Star 4-WAY reflecting bullets
      9. Giant spinning bullets spread into bullets
      10. High-speed scatter of blue-white and white bullets
      11. Blue-white bullet 5-WAY + 4-WAY
      12. Blue-white bullets radiating

      1~3 is Phase 1.
      4~6 is Phase 2.
      7~9 is Phase 3.
      Phase 4 is powered-up versions of 1~9.
      10~12 is Phase 5 (only up to Phase 5 on no-continue)

      Something like that.

      Of these, difficult ones are 4, 8, 12.
      ・For 4, gradually speeds up, so fast bullets overtake slow bullets from early on.
        First dodge fast bullets, then carefully dodge slow bullets.
      ・For 8, first receive 4-way directly under boss. Soon 4-way starts moving left and
        right, so move yourself matching it. Direction reverses midway, so change your
        direction with good timing too.
          When this attack comes in Phase 4, it's quite difficult. Fine to use bomb.
      ・For 12, only on no-continue, but bullets have quite narrow intervals, so if you
        don't lure boss to left or right before this attack, it's quite tight.
          First, lure to the edge.
